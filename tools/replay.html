    The driver

    A simulation, not a person: a learner who brakes far too early for the hairpin, creeps through turn 2, takes the wrong side of the road into the chicane and coasts out of turn 6. After each lap they do what the coach asked in the focus corner, and nothing else. Once nothing is being lost, the coach says "find the limit" and they use a little more of the car there.

    The coach

    The actual app code, version , running outside the game against a stand-in for it. Each drawing call the HUD made was recorded as it happened and is replayed here; the voice clips are the ones the game plays.

    What it is not

    Game footage, or proof about a human driver. The track is a 1.9 km test circuit made for the app's automated tests, and fonts differ from the game's. Red on the line is where the teacher brakes, green where it is flat out.
